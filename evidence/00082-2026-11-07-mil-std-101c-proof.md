## Primary-standard mapping

The published MIL-STD-101C PDF was read directly from `https://www.wbdg.org/FFC/FEDMIL/milstd101.pdf` with `pdftotext -layout`. Sections 4.2 and 4.3 assign:

| Name | FS chip | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Yellow | 13655 | flammable materials |
| Brown | 10080 | toxic and poisonous materials |
| Blue | 15102 | anesthetics and harmful materials |
| Green | 14187 | oxidizing materials |
| Gray | 16187 | physically dangerous materials |
| Red | 11105 | fire protection materials |
| Black | 17038 | no significant meaning |
| White | 17875 | no significant meaning |
| Orange | 12246 | no significant meaning; cylinder segregation |
| Buff | 13594 | no significant meaning; cylinder segregation |

The implementation contains exactly these ten keys and chips. Its test iterates every pair and requires the palette literal to equal `fs595c.colors['FS nnnnn']`.
